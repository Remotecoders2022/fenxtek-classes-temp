03 — Typing test

A real steno typing test, in the browser.

Three scripts, four durations, and results that don’t depend on which keyboard layout your laptop happens to be set to.

3scripts · 4durations

English, Kruti Dev and MangalQWERTY plain text, हिन्दी कृतिदेव (Kruti Dev 010) and हिन्दी मंगल (Inscript) — the three scripts the exams actually use.
15, 30, 60 or 120 secondsLive WPM, accuracy, error count and a running timer while you type.
A proper result cardNet and raw WPM, accuracy, consistency, keystrokes, errors and time — plus whether it’s a new personal best.
Personal bests per scriptYour best and your last 10 runs are stored on your account, with the E-coins each one earned.
Layout-independent scoringThe test reads physical key codes, so an Inscript or AZERTY system layout can’t inflate — or wreck — your score.
Earn while you practiseBeat your personal best above the academy’s threshold and E-coins land in your wallet automatically.

Built for people still learning the keys

Most typing tests assume you already know where every character sits. This one doesn’t.

  • On-screen keyboard that highlights the next key you need
  • Kruti Dev aware — the hint follows the cluster you’re building, and shows when Shift is needed
  • Devanagari is rendered properly for both Kruti Dev and Mangal
  • Restart, change script or change duration without losing your place

05 — Payments, EMI & receipts

Pay online, by UPI, or at the academy.

Three payment paths, one order record — and a receipt that looks like it came from a real institute.

3ways to pay

01

Online payment

Instant access, powered by Razorpay.

  • Cards, UPI, netbanking and wallets in one sheet
  • Name, email and phone pre-filled from the student profile
  • Course unlocks the moment payment succeeds
  • Switchable from the CRM — and only the Key ID is ever stored, never a secret
02

UPI QR & UPI ID

For students who’d rather pay from their banking app.

  • Scannable QR generated in the browser — no third-party image service
  • “Open UPI app” deep link for phones (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, any bank app)
  • Student pastes the UTR / transaction ID; the academy matches it in its bank app and approves
  • Offline too: pay at the desk or over a call, and the CRM records the sale
03

No-cost EMI

Split a course into parts — zero interest, zero fees.

  • Configurable number of parts and gap between them, per course or academy-wide
  • The course unlocks after part 1 and stays open while payments are on time
  • 5-day grace on every due date; access pauses automatically if a part is missed
  • The final part unlocks the full validity; each instalment is its own order with its own receipt
  • Reminders arrive as notifications before each due date
04

Orders & receipts

A proper paper trail for every rupee.

  • Order history with status, order number and method
  • Receipt as a PNG, drawn on canvas at 2× so it prints and shares crisply
  • Line items, discounts, E-coins spent and taxes — “prices include all taxes”
  • Refunds return any E-coins the student spent

Payment methods can be turned on and off from Payment settings in the CRM. If none is switched on, checkout tells the student to call or message the academy instead of failing silently.

07 — E-coins & referrals

Practise more, refer a friend, pay less.

1 E-coin = ₹1, spent at checkout like cash. Coins are only ever credited by a real action — never handed out by the browser.

1:1coin to rupee

01

Earning

Three ways in, all verified.

  • Typing personal bests above the academy’s WPM threshold
  • A welcome bonus for joining with someone’s referral code
  • A referral reward when your friend’s first order settles
  • An admin can also credit or void coins by hand, with a note and a reason
02

Spending

Applied at checkout, after every other discount.

  • Redeem up to a cap set by the academy (a % of the order)
  • Shown as its own line in the receipt and the order breakdown
  • If an order is rejected or refunded, the coins come back
03

Referral programme

Every student gets their own code and link.

  • Personal referral code, created automatically on your account
  • Share to WhatsApp, Telegram, copy the link, or use the phone’s native share sheet
  • A referral table showing who joined, whether it’s pending or rewarded, and how many coins it earned
  • Programme can be switched on or off, and tuned, from the CRM

The academy sets the coins-per-action, the welcome bonus, the minimum qualifying order and the maximum share of an order payable in coins. Wallet history shows every credit and debit with its reason.

11 — Platform & engineering

Boring, in the good way.

Static hosting, a real-time database and rules that decide who can read what — so the whole thing stays fast and cheap to run.

0servers to maintain

01

Fast by default

Static files, cache-busted, served from a CDN.

  • No server-side rendering to wait on
  • Images validated and sized before they’re stored
  • Scripts are plain ES modules — no framework, no build step
02

Security rules

Authorisation lives in the database, not the browser.

  • A student can only read and write their own orders, progress and wallet
  • Course content links are served from a separate public outline
  • Admin writes require an admin claim; no secret keys are ever stored in the database
  • Every rendered value is escaped
03

Accessibility

Usable by everyone, on everything.

  • Semantic landmarks, skip-friendly heading order and visible focus rings
  • aria-expanded, aria-current and role="dialog" where they belong
  • prefers-reduced-motion honoured across every animation
  • 44px touch targets and colour contrast checked on the dark sections
04

Real-time everywhere

Change it once, it changes for everyone.

  • Firebase Realtime Database — a price edit appears in every open portal
  • Live order, EMI and notification updates without a refresh
  • The academy’s edits and the student’s view share one code path, so they can’t drift apart

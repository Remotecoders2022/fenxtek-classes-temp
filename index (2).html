09 — FAQ

Asked often.

The practical details — speeds, transcription time, fonts and what a membership includes.

06answers
before you join

With 1–2 hours of daily practice, most students reach 80 WPM in 2–3 months and 100+ WPM in 6–12 months.

Grade C & D requires 100 WPM Hindi dictation (10 minutes) followed by 60 minutes of transcription on a computer.

The UPSSSC skill test allows 30 minutes. Our platform can be set to exactly this time for accurate preparation.

60 minutes to transcribe 400 words, with a 5% error formula — which our platform calculates automatically.

Mangal is Unicode — the standard for most government exams. KrutiDev is the legacy font still used in some AHC patterns. We support both, plus Remington Gail.

Unlimited access to all dictation courses, typing software, 400 to 1000 word passages, mock tests and progress tracking — from ₹549.

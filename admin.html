Payment settings

How students pay at checkout and what prints on their receipt. Stored in site/payments (public — never put secret keys here).

UPI (manual verification) Razorpay (instant)

Use the Key ID only — never the Key Secret. Without a server, Razorpay payments are recorded by the browser, so they show as Not verified until you tick them off against your Razorpay dashboard. For fully automatic verification add a Cloud Function webhook later.

Receipt details

If no online method is switched on, checkout asks students to call or message the academy — record those with Orders → Record offline sale.

Prices are treated as inclusive of all taxes.

Store theme Normal

Dress the student store up for festivals, like Blinkit does. Pick Diwali, Holi, Christmas, New Year or a holiday sale; customise the banner, colours, coupon and effects; and schedule it to start and end on its own. Students see it on the course store, course pages and cart.

Themes

E-coins & referrals Off

Every student gets a personal referral code. When a friend signs up with it and their first paid order is confirmed (you approve the UPI payment, verify the Razorpay payment or record an offline sale), both get E-coins. 1 E-coin = ₹1 off courses and memberships at checkout, like Flipkart SuperCoins.

Refer & earn

Paid when a friend joins with a student’s invite link or code and their first order is confirmed.

Purchase rewards

E-coins back on every paid order — like cashback. Set fixed coins for a single course under Courses → “E-coins on purchase”.

Redeeming
Stored in site/rewards. Coins are credited when you approve or verify a payment; refunds take them back.

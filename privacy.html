01Who we are

FenxTek Shorthand Classes (“FenxTek”, “we”, “us”) is a shorthand, typing and government-exam coaching academy based in Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh, India. We teach Hindi and English shorthand, dictation and typing (Mangal, KrutiDev and Remington Gail) through classroom batches and an online student portal.

This policy covers the FenxTek website, the student portal (sign-in, dashboard, course store, membership and receipts) and every enquiry you send us by phone, WhatsApp, email, Telegram, Instagram or the contact form.

02What we collect

We collect two kinds of information: what you give us directly, and what the portal records automatically while you study.

  • Account details — your name, email address, phone number and password credential (stored by our authentication provider, never in plain text). If an administrator creates an account for you, we store the same details.
  • Profile details you choose to add — target exam, current or goal typing speed, city and the photo or initials shown on your profile.
  • Enrolment and purchase records — courses bought, membership plans, order numbers, amounts, payment mode (UPI, gateway, E-coins, coupon or part payment), instalment due dates and receipt downloads.
  • Learning activity — which lessons, dictations and tests you open, progress ticks, watch time and the dates you accessed a course.
  • Enquiries and messages — anything you send through the contact form, WhatsApp, email, Telegram or Instagram, including leads we record for follow-up.
  • Device and technical data — browser type, screen size, approximate IP-based region, and the pages you open, used only to keep the portal working and to fix errors.
  • Notifications — if you switch on alerts, we store a per-device token so the academy can send class, payment and reminder notifications.
We do not collectcard numbers, CVV, UPI PINs, net-banking passwords, Aadhaar numbers, caste or religion data, or precise location. Please never share a PIN or password with anyone claiming to be from FenxTek.

04Payments, E-coins and offers

Course payments are collected through UPI, payment gateways or manual approval by the academy, and are recorded against your order in the portal.

  • Payment instruments. UPI IDs, card details and bank credentials are entered only in your UPI app or on the payment provider’s page. FenxTek never receives or stores them.
  • What we keep. The order amount, the payment mode, the reference or transaction ID you or the gateway shares, and the approval status.
  • E-coins. Referral rewards and wallet credits (1 E-coin = ₹1) are recorded against your account so they can be spent at checkout. Reversing a payment reverses the coins it earned.
  • Coupons and part payments. We keep a record of codes you used and any instalment plan attached to your order, so dues and reminders are accurate.
Verification.Payments marked “pending” stay pending until the academy confirms the money has arrived. We may contact you on the number or email linked to your account to confirm a payment.

05Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data, and we do not share it with advertising networks.

  • Service providers. Our portal runs on Google’s Firebase platform (authentication and database hosting) and our files are served over standard web infrastructure. These providers process data on our instructions only.
  • Payment partners. Your UPI app, bank or payment gateway receives the details needed to settle your payment.
  • Communication tools. Messages you send us by WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram or email are handled by those services under their own privacy policies.
  • Legal authorities. We may disclose records when required by law, court order or a lawful government request, or to protect the rights and safety of students and the academy.
  • Business changes. If the academy is ever reorganised, student records may move to the successor, which would remain bound by this policy.

06Cookies, storage and analytics

We keep cookies and browser storage deliberately minimal.

  • Essential storage — keeps you signed in, remembers your cart, remembers that you dismissed the introductory animation and saves preferences such as reduced motion or notification settings.
  • Preferences — your chosen alert settings and the last page you were on.
  • No advertising cookies — we do not run ad pixels or cross-site tracking on the portal.

You can clear site data in your browser at any time; you will simply be signed out. Because our pages load fonts and scripts from third-party networks (such as Google Fonts and our database provider), those providers may see standard request data such as your IP address.

07How long we keep your data

  • Account and profile — kept while your account is active, and for up to 24 months after you stop using the portal, after which it is deleted or anonymised.
  • Enrolment and access records — kept for the validity of the course plus 12 months, so we can restore access or resolve a dispute.
  • Orders, invoices and receipts — retained for at least 8 years where Indian tax and accounting rules require it.
  • Enquiries and leads — kept for up to 24 months unless you ask us to remove them sooner.
  • Backups — deleted data can persist in encrypted backups for up to 30 days before it is overwritten.

08How we protect it

We use sensible, layered safeguards for an academy of our size: access is protected by account authentication and database security rules, the portal is served over HTTPS, administrative access is limited to named staff, and payment approval is a manual, verified step.

No online service can promise perfect security. If a breach affecting your data ever occurs, we will tell you and the relevant authority as required by law, and explain what happened and what we are doing about it.

Do your part.Use a strong password, never share it, sign out on shared or cyber-café computers, and tell us immediately at Support.fenxtek@gmail.com if you think someone has used your account.

10Students under 18

Many of our aspirants are between 16 and 18 years old. We ask students below 18 to register with the knowledge of a parent or guardian, and we do not knowingly collect more information than a coaching academy needs from a minor. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has shared data with us that you would like removed, write to us and we will act on it.

11Other websites and apps

Our pages link to exam portals, WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, payment apps and other resources. Once you leave FenxTek, their own privacy policies apply — please read them before sharing information. We are not responsible for the practices of third-party sites.

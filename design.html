01 — Principles

Editorial, kinetic, exam-serious.

The site should feel like a well-set magazine about a skill — calm paper, confident ink, one bright accent, and motion that always has somewhere to go.

(01)

Warm paper, deep ink

Light pages sit on --paper, never pure white. Dark bands use --ink, never pure black.

(02)

Grotesk × italic serif

Inter Tight carries the message; one or two words switch to Instrument Serif italic for the voice.

(03)

Mono for metadata

JetBrains Mono, uppercase and widely tracked, labels numbers, eyebrows, chips and captions.

(04)

One ease, long tails

Everything moves on cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1) — fast start, soft landing.

Rules

How the pairing works
Do
  • Italicise one phrase per headline with <em> — it becomes serif automatically.
  • Violet italics on paper, lime italics on ink (.on-dark swaps it).
  • End display headlines with a full stop: “clear it.”
  • Use mono caps for numbers, labels and anything that reads like data.
Don’t
  • Set whole paragraphs in the serif or in mono.
  • Use bold serif, or serif without italics, in headlines.
  • Track body text, or loosen tracking on display sizes.
  • Mix violet and lime italics in the same section.

10 — Icons

One sprite, one stroke.

Line icons on a 24px grid with a 1.6–1.8 stroke, injected once by chrome.js. Use <svg><use href="#i-name"/></svg> — click an icon to copy it. Prefer icons over text glyphs (such as arrows) for UI controls.

11 — Keyboard wordmark

The name, typed out.

The footer signs off with FenxTek spelled on seven 3D keycaps — a nod to typing and shorthand. Hover a key to press it, move the mouse to tilt the board, or type F, E, N, X, T, K on your own keyboard.

(01)PlateDark aluminium gradient, 1px top highlight, solid edge shadow for thickness. Tilted 15° back.
(02)CapCream for F-E-N-X, lime for T-E-K. Side shading plus a two-tone wall drawn with box-shadow.
(03)DishLighter top surface set back toward the rear; a highlight follows the cursor.
(04)LegendInter Tight 600 on cream, Instrument Serif italic on lime, mono index 01–07.
(05)PressCap travels 82% of its wall in .16s; under-glow lights (lime / sky) while held.

Markup: chrome.js keyboard() · styles: .kb / .kk in site.css · behaviour: site.js
